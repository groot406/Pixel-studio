What you get

Everything a sprite needs, from the first pixel to the game.

Draw like a pixel artist

Pencil, curves, shapes, dither and shading along your palette's own colour ramps. Mirror drawing, a magic wand and a palette studio keep every pixel deliberate.

Animate with frames and bones

A timeline with onion skins, plus skeleton rigs with ready-made walk, run, jump, idle and wave motions. Squash and stretch without redrawing every frame.

AI that edits pixels Pro

Ask the assistant for changes, review the result and apply it, with Undo. Generate a sprite from a description and Pixel Studio snaps it to a clean grid and your palette.

Objects and reusable parts

Split artwork into objects, group them and reuse parts across frames. Change a part once and every frame that uses it follows.

Export for any engine

Crisp PNGs at any scale and PNG sequences. On Pro: sprite sheets with Aseprite, Godot and Unity metadata, animated GIF and APNG.

Clean up AI images

Images from general AI models only look like pixel art. The importer finds the hidden pixel grid and turns them into real, editable pixels.

How it works

From idea to sprite sheet in three steps.

  1. Open the editor. It runs in your browser and your projects are saved on your device as you work.
  2. Draw, or describe it. Start from a blank canvas, an example, an imported image or an AI sketch.
  3. Animate and export. Add frames or a bone rig, then export PNGs, sprite sheets or GIFs.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. The editor works without one and keeps your projects in your browser. Sign in when you want a cloud copy of a project or Pro features.

What are AI credits?

AI requests cost us money to run, so Pro includes 1,000 credits every month. A typical assistant reply uses 2–10 credits, an AI edit 5–40 and a generated image 6–84, depending on quality. Unused monthly credits don't roll over; extra credit packs never expire.

Which file formats can I export?

Free exports PNG and WebP frames, PNG sequences and project files (.pxa, .pxs). Pro adds sprite sheets with Aseprite, Godot 4 or Unity metadata, animated GIF and APNG.

Can I import my existing sprites?

Yes. Drop PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF images onto the canvas, or import sprite sheets. Pixel Studio project files open directly.

How do I cancel?

Open your account in the editor and choose Billing & invoices. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then your account moves to Free. Your projects stay.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. Your account page lets you download everything we store and delete your account with its cloud projects.
